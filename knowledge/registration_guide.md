# SDU Portal Course Registration Guide

# **Overview**

Course registration takes place every semester on [my.sdu.edu.kz](https://my.sdu.edu.kz/) under Academic Operations → Course Registration. No credit is awarded for a course that has no registration record in the portal.

The official Portal Guideline available in the portal is the 2021 [Student Handbook](https://my.sdu.edu.kz/common/download/handbook.pdf) (46 pages). It contains only a few general registration rules and no step-by-step instructions. This guide was therefore compiled using the portal pages themselves.

# **Before Registration**

The registration window opens once per semester and remains open for only 9–12 days. Choose your courses in advance.

* Go to Information → System Calendar, select the semester, and check the dates in the “Course Registration (for students)” row. The Individual Permissions table below shows your personal registration window, which may open later than the general registration period.  
* Your study plan is listed by semester under Academic Operations → My Curriculum. Electives are marked with an “XXX” code, with the available course codes shown in parentheses. “Available” in the Status column means you can take the course; “IP” in the grade field means the course is currently in progress.  
* Discuss your chosen courses with your advisor in advance. Your advisor’s name appears on the portal home page.  
* Check your balance under Information → MY Accounting Info. The Course Registration page displays the price of each course.

Registration Stages for Fall 2026

Registration closes before classes begin; only adjustment periods remain afterward. Academic year 2026–2027, fall semester, according to System Calendar.

| Stage | Dates in 2026 |
| :---- | :---- |
| Course Registration | August 10–21 |
| Classes begin | September 1 |
| Add \- Drop | September 7–13 |
| Late registration (fee required) | September 14–19 |
| Early withdrawal | September 21 – October 17 |
| Late withdrawal | October 19 – December 12 |

Some periods close at 00:00 on the listed final date. For example, Early withdrawal ends at 00:00 on October 17, so the last day to withdraw is October 16\. The spring semester follows the same sequence: in 2026, registration ran from January 8–16, and Add-Drop ran from January 26 to February 1\.

# **Wish List for Advance Planning**

The Wish list is a planning list for the upcoming semester; it does not register you for courses. You must still register through Course Registration within the dates shown in the calendar.

1. Open Academic Operations → Wish list.  
2. In the Wish List basket section, select the semester, for example, 2026 \- 2027 Spring.  
3. In the Course structure table below, click add next to the course. For an elective group, click Choose and select a specific course from that group.  
4. The total ECTS and the limit are shown above the basket: 40 ECTS for fall and spring, and 20 ECTS for summer.  
5. To remove an unwanted course, click remove in the OPERATION column.

# **Course Registration Step by Step**

When your window opens, go to Academic Operations → Course Registration. If the window is closed, the page displays “Course registration is CLOSED”.

1. Check your program. It normally shows “M \- Main Track”. Only if you have a second program should you select “Check to switch program” and switch using the list.  
2. Find the course. Select a search type and click Show, or enter the course code in the search field. Electives appear under their respective groups.  
3. Select the sections. First select the N (Normal) section, which is the lecture section. Its linked P (practice) and L (laboratory) sections will then appear; select one of each.  
4. Check the timetable. Selected sections immediately appear in the Course schedule grid below. The table below explains the colors.  
5. Add the course. Click the add button and confirm “Are you sure about adding selected section?”. For an elective, first identify the elective group.  
6. Remove a course if necessary. When you delete a course from the list, the portal asks “Cancel selected section?”.  
7. Confirm your registration. After adding all courses, click the confirmation button and confirm “Do you want your selected courses to be approved?”. The status changes to STUDENT CONFIRMED; approved registration is displayed as APPROVED on the page.  
8. Check the final list. The table shows the course code, N/P/L sections, cr (credits), ECTS, status (“First time” means taking the course for the first time), and price. The last row shows the total ECTS.

| Timetable color | Label | Meaning |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Light green | In basket | The course has been added to your list |
| Light blue | Available | The section can be selected |
| Blue | Selected | The section you are currently selecting |
| Pink | Unavailable | Conflicts with another course; cannot be added |

# **Consent Requests**

Some sections require permission to register, for example, if you have not completed a prerequisite. Courses with prerequisites are marked PR in the requisites column on the My Curriculum page.

1. On the Course Registration page, select the course’s N section. If permission is required, a message form appears.  
2. Briefly explain why you want to take the course. The request cannot be sent if the field is empty.  
3. Confirm “Do you really want to send a request?”. “Sent successfully\!” means the request has been sent.  
4. Track the response under Academic Operations → Consent Requests.

Possible portal responses:

* “You have already sent request” — your request has already been sent; wait for a response.  
* “You were refused” — your request was declined; choose another section or course.  
* “Consent Request NOT AVAILABLE for this section” — permission requests are not available for this section.

# **After Registration**

After registration closes, there are three periods for changing your course list. Their dates are shown in the registration stages table above.

* Add \- Drop — the first week of classes. You can add or remove courses and change sections.  
* Apply for Late Course Registration — for students who did not register on time. The portal warns that this application requires a fee, the payment is non-refundable, and the application cannot be withdrawn.  
* Early / Late withdrawal — withdrawing from a course through Academic Operations → Withdrawals. Speak to your advisor before making a decision.

Your final timetable is available under Academic Operations → Course Schedule and at the bottom of the Course Registration page. Monitor your attendance through Electronic Attendance.

# **Rules and Limits**

You can take 20–40 ECTS per semester; 30 ECTS is recommended. The limit is calculated using ECTS, not the cr (credits) column.

| Rule | Value | Source |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Semester workload | 20–40 ECTS; 30 recommended | [Student Handbook](https://my.sdu.edu.kz/common/download/handbook.pdf), 2021 |
| Planning limit | Fall and spring: 40 ECTS; summer: 20 ECTS | Wish list page |
| Unregistered course | No credit awarded | [Student Handbook](https://my.sdu.edu.kz/common/download/handbook.pdf), 2021 |
| Attendance | Missing 30% of course hours results in failing the course | [Student Handbook](https://my.sdu.edu.kz/common/download/handbook.pdf), 2021 |
| Late registration | Fee required; non-refundable | Course Registration page |
| Prerequisite | Marked PR on My Curriculum | My Curriculum page |

For the conditions governing Add-Drop, withdrawal, and late registration, see Regulation on the credit system-based teaching process on the Rules and Regulations page: [Kazakh](https://files.sdu.edu.kz/pms/rule-attachments/0KPN5EtRQ6ZpyRdjVA5ahcBdd8OgpVOE8jOJHbC1.pdf), [English](https://files.sdu.edu.kz/pms/rule-attachments/WNJyGU00oPPYhpCC6XfJJRSZQfwpl5z4WUjQyBrr.pdf).

# **Common Portal Messages**

The portal displays errors in short English pop-up messages. The most common are listed below:

| Message | Meaning | What to do |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Unavailable sections selected\! | The section conflicts with another course in your timetable | Choose another section |
| Normal section of course is not identified\! | No N section selected | Select the N section first |
| Practice section of subject is not identified. | No P section selected | Select a practice section |
| LAB section of subject is not identified. | No L section selected | Select a laboratory section |
| Group of elective course is not identified\! | The elective group has not been identified | Specify which elective slot the course will fill |
| Search criteria is empty. | The search field is empty | Enter the course code |
| Please fill message field | No reason provided in the consent request | Fill in the message field |

For questions about course selection, consent, and withdrawal, contact your advisor first. Administrative matters are handled by the Student Service Centre. If you cannot log in, click “Can't access my account” on the login page.
