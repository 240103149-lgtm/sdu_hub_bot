## Grades
- Final grades use a 100-point scale. Passing grades are A to D; failing grades are FX and F. [R cl. 35]
- Final course grade = current assessment 60% + interim assessment (exam session) 40%. [R cl. 36]
- Letter scale [H]: A 95–100 (4.0), A- 90–94 (3.67), B+ 85–89 (3.33), B 80–84 (3.0), B- 75–79 (2.67), C+ 70–74 (2.33), C 65–69 (2.0), C- 60–64 (1.67), D+ 55–59 (1.33), D 50–54 (1.0), FX 25–49 (0), F 0–24 (0). Courses excluded from the GPA are graded P (Pass, 50–100%) or NP (Not Pass, 0–49%).
- GPA is calculated on a 4.0 scale, weighted by the credits of each course. [F]

## Attendance
Students must attend at least 70% of all scheduled classes. Otherwise they are not admitted to the final exam and get F for the course. In exceptional cases the Faculty/School may change the 70% requirement for a course. [R cl. 34(9)]

## Study load
- 20–40 ECTS per term; at least 60 ECTS per academic year is recommended. [R cl. 34] (The Tuition fees page says 20–42 ECTS; the Regulation is the rule.)
- A student with fewer than 20 ECTS left in the final term registers only for the remaining credits. [R cl. 34]
- A course without a registration record gives no credit. [H]

## Add/Drop and withdrawal
- Registration period and Add/Drop week: a course can be dropped, replaced or added free of charge. [R cl. 34(7), F]
- Early withdrawal (after Add/Drop, up to the 7th week): pay 50% of the course fee. [R cl. 34(8)]
- Late withdrawal (after the 7th week): pay 100% of the course fee. [R cl. 34(8)]
- Outside these periods a registered course cannot be dropped and must be completed. [R cl. 34(8)]
- Use the dates in sdu_academic_calendar_2026_2027.md; the week numbers printed in the SSC FAQ do not match them, so confirm with ssc@sdu.edu.kz if in doubt.

## FX, F, AW and retakes
- FX (final grade 25–49): the student may retake the final assessment without repeating the course. [R cl. 35, F]
- How to apply: press "Order FX" in the portal (my.sdu.edu.kz → Grade List) within the FX application dates. The fee equals the price of 1 ECTS and is deducted automatically, so the account must have enough money first. [F]
- F (0–24): the student must register for the course again and complete all its work and the final assessment. [R cl. 35]
- AW is given for academic reasons (for example breaking exam rules) and withdraws the student from the course; FX is not allowed for it. Three AW grades lead to expulsion. [F]
- A retake course is opened only if at least five students register. [R cl. 34(4)]

## Summer term
- Lasts at least six weeks and is not offered to final-year students. [R cl. 22]
- All summer courses are paid; the usual discounts and benefits do not apply. [R cl. 22.2, F]
- A course opens with at least 5 Bachelor's (3 Master's) students. Late registration and dropping a course are not allowed in summer. Maximum 20 ECTS. [R cl. 22.1–22.2]

## Academic standing
- Students are promoted to the next year automatically. [R cl. 36.2] A student who does not finish the programme on time repeats the final year. [R cl. 36.4]
- Expulsion: GPA below 1 at the end of the first year; after that GPA is checked every semester and a GPA below 1 leads to expulsion. [F]

## Scholarship and allowances
- To keep the scholarship the student must score 70 or above in each subject, i.e. no final grade below C+. [F]
- Losing it in the fall (winter session) stops payments for February–August; losing it in the spring stops payments for September–January. [F]
- A State Educational Grant is kept for the whole programme duration. [R cl. 36.3]
- Travel allowance: paid twice a year (winter and summer vacations) regardless of academic results, equal to 4 MCI; graduating students do not get the summer payment. [F]
- Exact scholarship amounts and the Tuition Fee Regulation are not in this knowledge base: refer to accounting@sdu.edu.kz.

## Diploma with honours (red diploma)
GPA at least 3.5, final grades from A to C+, and A or A- for the comprehensive exam and/or thesis. Not awarded to a student who retook interim assessments. [F]

## Academic leave, transfers, military department
- If an absence for a valid reason is longer than five weeks, apply to the SSC for academic leave. [R cl. 34]
- Grant students may take academic leave for health reasons, childbirth or military service (documents required); fee-paying students also for financial, family and other reasons. [F]
- Transfer to another SDU programme keeps the grant only if both programmes have the same qualification; changes happen during the summer or winter break. Transfer to another university: the application must say "with retention of the state grant". Students transferring in may need to take difference courses for a fee. [F]
- Military Department: voluntary and fee-paying; applications open in February of the first year; Reserve Lieutenant takes 2 years, Junior Reserve Sergeant 1 year; training is held at partner universities (the SSC page names KazNU, Narxoz University and KazNAU, among others). [F]
