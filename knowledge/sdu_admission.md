# SDU Bachelor's admission 2026 (түсу, қабылдау / поступление, приём)

Source: https://sdu.edu.kz/en/admission-3-2/ — checked 18.10.2026.
The full list of documents is in the "required_documents.json" section of this knowledge base (the same list as the bot's /documents checklist). When a student asks which documents are needed to enter or enroll at SDU, give that list for their track (state grant or tuition-based).

## How to apply
1. Register and fill in the application on https://admissions.sdu.edu.kz . It must be completed before visiting the Admissions Office; print it and bring it.
2. Citizens of Kazakhstan applying for a state grant can also use "APPLY ONLINE FOR STATE GRANT" (enroll.sdu.edu.kz).
3. Bring the documents both electronically and on paper, originals and copies. Photos: 3×4 cm, 6 copies.
The bot's /documents is a student-project prototype: documents sent there are not officially submitted to SDU.

## Requirements
- School graduates are admitted on UNT (ҰБТ / ЕНТ) results. The minimum passing scores are only linked on the page, not written, so they are not in this knowledge base.
- College graduates applying for a state grant: at least 35 UNT points overall and at least 5 points in each subject. College graduates applying on a tuition basis also pass an interview.
- English (for programmes taught in English): IELTS overall 5.5 with at least 5.0 in each part, or SDU Language School level B1 or higher, or the SDU English exam (an offline level test).
- Applicants who completed compulsory military service also bring a copy of the military ID.
