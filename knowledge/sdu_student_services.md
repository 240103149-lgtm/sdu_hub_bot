# SDU student services: references, student ID card, documents, contacts

Sources, checked 18.10.2026: SSC / Office of the Registrar FAQ https://sdu.edu.kz/en/student-service-center-2/ ; Students page https://sdu.edu.kz/en/students/ ; Tuition fees page https://sdu.edu.kz/en/tuition-fees/
This file is newer than SDU_01–SDU_03 (checked 19.09.2026). The portal is my.sdu.edu.kz; its inner pages need a login and are not described here except in registration_guide.md.

## Student Service Center (SSC) and Advising Desk
- Open on weekdays 08:30–17:30. Email: ssc@sdu.edu.kz.
- Replies to letters sent to the administration take 3–5 working days.
- The SSC handles registration, confirmation of study, academic records, reinstatement and diplomas.

## References (справка / анықтама)
- Online: MY SDU portal → Online Services. The reference has a QR code and a digital signature and can be downloaded from the portal within 3 business days.
- Paper: in the same form choose "ADVISING DESK with a signature and stamp" and collect it at the Advising Desk.
- Types: for benefits (GOV.kz) forms №2, №4, №6; for military registration form №3; "Information about studying place" (for a school or an employer); transcript.

## Student ID card
1. Pay 2000 KZT: MY SDU portal → Accounting Info (Kaspi QR and Halyk QR are also accepted).
2. Apply: MY SDU portal → Online Services → Student Card, delivery method "Advising Desk".
3. The card is ready in 3–5 working days. Collect it at the Advising Desk on working days 13:30–15:30.

## Documents for graduates
- Transcripts and archival documents are issued only to graduates: fill in the Google form (QR code on the SSC page) or email a copy of the ID/passport to ssc@sdu.edu.kz with the document needed, its language and the graduation year. Ready in 3 working days; an email is sent when it is ready.
- Lost diploma: request a duplicate through eGov or a Public Service Center (ЦОН / ХҚКО). Free, ready in 15 working days. A lost transcript needs a separate duplicate-transcript request.

## Tuition payment
- Tuition is paid before the semester starts; the amount depends on the ECTS of the chosen courses. Ways to pay: https://sdu.edu.kz/epay/ , https://ems.sdu.edu.kz , Kaspi (Education → Universities), or bank transfer (bank details are in the "Реквизиты" section of the Tuition fees page). The balance and course prices are in the portal (Information → MY Accounting Info).
- Credits needed to graduate: Bachelor's 240 ECTS, Master's 120, PhD 180.
- Tuition amounts are published only as PDF files on the Tuition fees page and are not in this knowledge base. Do not name a price; send the student to https://sdu.edu.kz/en/tuition-fees/ or accounting@sdu.edu.kz.

## IT help
- Portal problems: open a ticket at helpdesk.sdu.edu.kz (submit a request → my portal).
- Moodle password recovery: password.sdu.edu.kz. Portal password help: ssc@sdu.edu.kz, or "Can't access my account" on the portal login page.
