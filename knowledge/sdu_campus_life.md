## Student House (dormitory, жатақхана, общежитие), 2026–2027
| Fee | Per year | Per semester |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Accommodation | 552,500 KZT | 276,250 KZT |
| Meal plan | 586,500 KZT | 293,250 KZT |
| Total | 1,139,000 KZT | 569,500 KZT |
- Pay in full for a semester or for the year; installments through Kaspi Red.
- Included: breakfast and dinner, room cleaning, medical services, linen change and laundry every two weeks, bedding, filtered water, free Wi-Fi, 24/7 security and reception, sports courts, billiards, table tennis, campus events.
- Extra, paid: gym, hair salon, shop, self-service laundry 550 KZT per wash (drying is free).
- Apply: "Registration" at https://dorm.sdu.edu.kz/dashboard . Send the documents to dormservice.boys@mail.ru (boys) or dormservice.girls@mail.ru (girls).
- Documents: 3×4 photos, a copy of the ID card, a copy of the payment receipt and a copy of a medical certificate. The page has two lists that disagree: one asks for medical form 075, the other for form №086, and the number of photos differs (2 or 4). Tell the student to confirm with the dorm office before sending.
- House rules in the 2021 handbook: curfew hours for boys and girls "6:00 am to 10:00 pm", lights out at 23:00. The current website does not publish house rules, so these may be outdated.
- Campus entry (2021 handbook): the guard asks for an identity card, a student ID card or the MY SDU portal home page.

## Medical Center (медпункт, медициналық орталық, медицинский центр) — yes, SDU has one on campus
- Location: G block, 1st floor, room 109/110. There is also a point in the Student House, D block, 1st floor.
- Phone: +7 (747) 716 60 75, ext. 248 (09:00–18:00). Student House line, 24 hours: +7 (778) 997 58 39. Email: medpunkt@sdu.edu.kz.
- The Social Affairs page gives Medical Center and Psychology Center hours as Monday–Friday 10:00–12:00 and 13:00–16:30.
- Medical certificates (to excuse missed classes):
  - Bring the certificate within 5 working days after it was issued.
  - The gap between the issue date and the recovery date must be no more than 3 working days.
  - It must show: certificate number, medical organization (name, stamp), doctor (full name, stamp), diagnosis and a QR code.
  - Hospital treatment: form 037/e. Illness longer than 21 days: form 037/e plus form 026/u (VKK, medical advisory commission).
  - Ambulance: the ambulance call sheet, call time between 09:00 and 18:00. Dentist: a certificate valid for 1 to 5 days.
- Fluorography: an annual check is mandatory. Bring the image with its description to the Medical Center every year when the previous one expires; students who are late are blocked from the portal automatically.

## Psychological support (психолог, психологиялық көмек)
- Free individual counseling in Kazakh, Russian and English, offline or online, up to 6 consultations per semester; also group therapy, seminars and crisis counseling.
- Booking: online appointment link on Taplink in the bio of https://www.instagram.com/sdu_psy/ . To cancel, email your psychologist with the subject "Appointment Cancellation".
- 24/7 helplines: 150 (national helpline for children and youth; WhatsApp +7 708 10 608 10) and 111 (family, women's and children's rights).

## Clubs and student life
- More than 28 student clubs and organizations in four directions: sport, entertainment and self-improvement, charity, education (Social Affairs page).
- How to join is not described on the website. Clubs present themselves at the Club Fair (see SDU FEST in campus_events.md); news is on Instagram @sdulife.

## Careers
- One-to-one guidance, skills sessions, employer presentations; 7 major career events per year, including Career Day (meet employers, mock interviews, chance of an internship or job) and a yearly Job Fair.
- Contacts: career@sdu.edu.kz, https://www.instagram.com/career.sdu , https://t.me/careersdu .

## Inclusion support
- For students with special educational needs: advice, a personal support plan, academic adjustments (class format, exam format, deadlines), assistive technology, accessible materials.
- How: email or visit the Educational-Methodical Center (emc@sdu.edu.kz) or the inclusion specialists in office H107; bring documents about the condition if possible.

## International students
- Migration registration within 3 working days (details: https://sdu.edu.kz/en/student-dependent-visa/ ).
- SDU cooperates with 47 partner universities. Exchange: Bachelor's students may apply from the second semester of the second year, Master's from the second semester of the first year, PhD from the second year; graduating students cannot apply.
- Students studying in English can join double-degree programmes with European partners.
- Contacts: iro@sdu.edu.kz (international relations), ssc@sdu.edu.kz (study questions).

## Library
Scientific Library: https://library.sdu.edu.kz/ , library@sdu.edu.kz. Loan limits, return dates and fines are not published on the website: say this information is not available and suggest asking the library.
